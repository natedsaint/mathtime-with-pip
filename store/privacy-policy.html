Mathtime With Pip Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Mathtime With Pip does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

What the app keeps

To show your best times, best scores, and recent tries, Mathtime With Pip saves a small file on your device. It also remembers your last game settings and whether sound is on. This information:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is not linked to your name or any account,
  • is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No internet connection is needed or used.
  • No camera, microphone, location, or contacts access.

Children

Mathtime With Pip is designed for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Parents do not need to provide any information for their child to use it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
